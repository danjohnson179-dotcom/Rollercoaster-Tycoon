Toxicator operator manual

From closed ride to dispatch

  1. Press K for control power. Guests arrive gradually only after O opens the queue; demand rises and falls in park waves.
  2. For an empty engineering run, select Empty test cycle. This closes the public queue and permits a dispatch with no riders.
  3. Press G to open the load gate, then A to release exactly one waiting batch. New arrivals remain behind the batch gate for the next cycle.
  4. Close the gate with G, then press R and wait for every occupied restraint circuit to prove.
  5. Confirm the platform with C, enable the drive with D, then hold SPACE.
  6. For manual mode, release arm lock with L and hold the arrow keys to drive the arms. Brake positions are persistent: Z RELEASED, X HALF and B FULL.
  7. FULL captures the gondola relative to the arms. RELEASED gives a true free swing. HALF adds friction without locking, so it can calm an excessive swing or shape the next inversion.
  8. Press S once to finish. The return controller brakes the ride, parks the arms, levels the gondola and proves the load locks automatically.
  9. Open restraints, then open the gate to unload. Once the platform is clear, press A to admit the next batch when you are ready.
  10. Aquafun can run while the ride is idle or moving: W starts the pump, P changes pattern and [/] adjusts jet height. The three zone isolators are on the motion desk.
  11. When a fault latches, open Maintenance or press M. Call the mechanic, select the affected system, complete the repair, then press F.
