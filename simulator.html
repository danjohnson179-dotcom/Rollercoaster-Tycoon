Toxicator operator manual

From closed ride to dispatch

  1. Press K for control power, then O to open the public entrance.
  2. Press G to open the load gate. Guests board one seat at a time from the queue.
  3. Close the gate with G, then press R and wait for every occupied restraint circuit to prove.
  4. Confirm the platform with C, enable the drive with D, then hold SPACE.
  5. For manual mode, release arm lock with L. Hold the arrow keys to drive the arms and press B to release or capture the free-swinging gondola.
  6. Press S to finish. A manual cycle only completes when arms and gondola are upright, stationary and both locks are engaged.
  7. Open restraints, then open the gate to unload. The next group boards automatically.
